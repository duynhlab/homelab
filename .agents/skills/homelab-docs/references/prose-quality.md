# Prose quality

Apply this pass after the claims and the structure are correct. The goal is
clear, natural platform documentation, not a generic rewrite.

## Preserve intent

- Preserve the supported meaning, facts, nuance and necessary uncertainty.
- Keep established homelab terms even when a synonym would add variety.
- Leave strong sentences alone. Make the minimum effective edit.
- Keep useful detail instead of replacing it with a broad summary.
- Do not invent examples, measurements, sources, opinions or implementation
  details.
- Match the neutral instructional voice of the page, not an individual's
  casual voice.

## Improve clarity

- Lead with the point when the setup adds no necessary context.
- Prefer concrete nouns and direct verbs over abstractions and inflated
  claims.
- Cut repetition, filler and empty qualifiers without deleting a real
  constraint.
- Untangle a sentence that needs rereading; keep a clear long sentence that
  carries necessary detail.
- Vary rhythm only when repeated sentence shapes sound mechanical.

## Warning signs

Treat these as warnings, not bans. Keep a construction when it is the clearest
choice or matches the page.

- **Unsupported importance:** pivotal, transformative, critical, without an
  evidenced consequence.
- **Promotional language:** seamless, robust, powerful, best-in-class;
  replace with the concrete behaviour.
- **Superficial analysis:** trailing "highlighting" or "underscoring"
  clauses that explain no mechanism or consequence.
- **Vague attribution:** "many teams", "experts agree", "studies show";
  name the source or remove the claim.
- **Stock rhetoric:** throat-clearing openers, self-answered questions,
  decorative contrasts ("not X, but Y"), negative lists, colon reveals when
  the statement works alone.
- **Decorative rhythm:** stacked fragments, forced groups of three, false
  ranges, the same sentence template repeated, punctuation for emphasis.
- **Mechanical diction:** fake-strong verbs, synonym cycling, swollen
  phrases, stacked hedging, generic AI vocabulary.
- **Chatbot artifacts:** canned praise, offers to continue, "I hope this
  helps", knowledge-cutoff disclaimers.
- **Weak endings:** a closing summary, slogan or metaphor after the last
  useful fact. End on the fact or the next action.
- **Decorative formatting:** scattered bold, unnecessary micro-headings, a
  list that reads better as two sentences.
- **Ease claims:** easy, simple, quick, just, simply. They state nothing the
  reader can verify.

## Final read

Read the changed section in full, then check:

1. Every claim still matches its source.
2. The revision preserves the original intent and necessary uncertainty.
3. Each sentence adds information, reasoning or a necessary transition.
4. The prose reads as direct and natural when read aloud.
5. The edit did not normalize unrelated or already clear writing.
